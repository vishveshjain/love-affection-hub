## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Personalized Onboarding & Photo Studio**:
  - Automatically prompts on first visit for who is interacting (Boyfriend or Girlfriend).
  - Pre-filled with Vishvesh & Laura.
  - Photo upload studio with instant previews and couple frames.
- 💋 **Dynamic Animated Affection Stage**:
  - **Give a kiss to Vishvesh / Laura**: Ballistic flying kiss trajectory, lipstick stamp mark on partner's photo, blush, sound effects, and confetti.
  - **Hug with Animated Embracing Arms**: Interactive arms extend from both photo cards to wrap around each other in a warm, tight double-embrace.
  - **Put my angel on your arms (Princess Carry)**: Supportive lifting arms scoop Laura into the air with stars and celebratory banners.
  - **Hold my angel**: Clasping hands bound by a pulsing golden & rose thread of destiny.
  - **Feed sweet treats to my angels**: Strawberries, cupcakes, and chocolates fly across into the partner's mouth with cute bite sparkles.
  - **Randomized Sweet Whispers**: 25+ romantic whispers + full browsable sentence drawer.
- 💬 **Live Couple Chat**:
  - Real-time cross-tab sync via BroadcastChannel & LocalStorage.
  - Love emojis bar, message reactions, and high-voltage **Love Buzz** vibration effect.
- 🌙 **Telling Dreams Journal**:
  - Categorized into "Dreams I Had About You Last Night" 🌙 and "Our Future Dreams Together" 🌟.
- 📝 **Love Notes & Loving Journey Roadmap**:
  - Daily pastel sticky love notes.
  - Relationship milestone roadmap with progress tracking and celebratory confetti.
- 🎮 **Love Games Suite**:
  - 🔥 **Animated FLAMES Calculator** with letter strike-outs, percentage meter, and love advice.
  - 🎟️ **Scratch-Off Love Coupons** with HTML5 canvas scratch cards and custom coupon creator.
  - 🎡 **Affection Spin Wheel** for cute couple dares and sweet duties.
  - 🧠 **Couple Chemistry Quiz** ("Who Is More Likely To...?").
  - 📸 **Polaroid Memory Wall & Scrapbook**.
- 🎵 **Web Audio Synthesizer**: Zero external MP3 dependencies for smooches, hugs, chimes, giggles, and dreamy lofi romance chord loops.
